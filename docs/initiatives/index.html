Upcoming initiatives

What we are building next.

Two initiatives in development that extend the group beyond collection and processing — one for residents and councils, one for everyone who recycles. Both are in development; neither is claimed as live.

no.comCouncil collection booking, online
In development

An online booking portal for council collection programs. Residents book a hard-waste or ad hoc collection, choose a collection window and receive confirmation. The council sees bookings, the schedule and completion. Each collection is recorded and reported by stream, so a council program produces the same diversion evidence as any other Recycle Group program.

  1. 01Resident books online — address, items, window, confirmation.
  2. 02Council sees the schedule, status and completion for every booking.
  3. 03Crew collects and records the load; material enters the group's chain of custody.
  4. 04Report — collections and diversion by stream, per program and period.
Recycle CreditsRewards for verified recycling
In development

A recycling-rewards platform. Members recycle at an accredited recycler and show a personal QR code; the recycler verifies the item, and verified recycling issues Recycle Credits to the member's wallet. Credits unlock offers funded by retail partners. Credits are a reward unit, not cash. The QR identifies the member, never the item, and carries no personal information.

  1. 01Recycle at an accredited recycler and show your QR.
  2. 02Verify — the recycler confirms the item and the credits are issued.
  3. 03Redeem credits against retail-partner offers.
  4. 04Pilot planned at the group's own North Geelong recycling operation.

Names, timing and partners for both initiatives are to be confirmed before public release.
