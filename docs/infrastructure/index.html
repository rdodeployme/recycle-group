Our infrastructure

Owned, not subcontracted.

Recycling programs are only as good as the facilities behind them. These are ours — transfer, processing, reuse and collection under one group.

01

Resource recovery centres

Recycle Resource Recovery Centres · 116 Furner Avenue, North Geelong VIC

90+
material streams separated on site

A purpose-built resource recovery centre designed for landfill diversion: undercover, open six days a week, with material separated into more than 90 streams on site ready for downstream recycling.

02

Mattress recycling facility

The Mattress Recycling Company

250k+
mattresses a year

Mattresses stripped to steel, foam, timber and fibre, with the steel recovered and returned to manufacturing. More than half of Victoria's discarded mattresses pass through the facility.

03

Polystyrene plant

Styro

41,580m³
EPS saved from landfill in the last 12 months

Expanded polystyrene densified and reprocessed into new polystyrene products. Licensed for the export of processed EPS.

04

Cardboard, timber & metal

Baling, containerisation and export

Export
baled, containerised and sold as commodities

Cardboard baled and containerised for export, timber and pallets recovered, metals recovered from the sort line and mattress steel and exported as commodities.

05

Reuse warehouse & collection

Recycle Warehouse · Melbourne · Brisbane

2
metropolitan collection markets, with further markets listed when live

Collection and clearance crews operate in Melbourne and Brisbane through the group's collection brands, with reuse retail and community redistribution run from the group's own warehouse operations.

06

Landfill last

Nunjara · Langi Ghiran Recycling

EPA
registered sites, every load recorded

The end of the chain, used last. Every inbound load is recorded by company, vehicle, origin, material and volume, so even what reaches landfill is counted rather than assumed.
