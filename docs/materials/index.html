The Mattress Recycling Company

Mattresses

Victoria was landfilling 160,000 mattresses a year. The Mattress Recycling Company recovers them at a 99% recovery rate: every mattress is stripped to steel, foam, timber and fibre. The steel is baled and exported as a commodity; per 10,000 mattresses around 270 tonnes of steel come out of 800 tonnes in, with 99% of that steel recovered. Shredded residual is a quarter of its original volume — less landfill space and better compaction.

Styro

Polystyrene (EPS)

Expanded polystyrene is up to 98% air. Styro densifies it into blocks a fraction of the size and reprocesses it for products such as picture frames, cornices and laminated timber — and exports processed EPS under the group's own licence. Australia generates around 45,000 tonnes of polystyrene a year.

Cardboard & paper

Cardboard is separated at the sort line, baled on the group's own balers and conveyors, containerised and exported. Retail, ports and logistics generate it by the tonne; the group turns it into a commodity rather than a cost.
