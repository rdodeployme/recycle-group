Materials

Nothing here is “away”.

What happens to each material stream after the weighbridge — where it goes, what it becomes and what the group can show for it.

01

MattressesThe Mattress Recycling Company

Every mattress is stripped to steel, foam, timber and fibre. The steel is baled and exported as a commodity; per 10,000 mattresses around 270 tonnes of steel come out of 800 tonnes in, with 99% of that steel recovered. Shredded residual is a quarter of its original volume — less landfill space and better compaction.

Processed12,000–30,000 a month
Steel contentabout 30% by weight
Time per mattressabout 38 seconds
Steel recovered99%
Residual volume25% of original
02

Polystyrene (EPS)Styro

Expanded polystyrene is up to 98% air. Styro densifies it into blocks a fraction of the size and reprocesses it for products such as picture frames, cornices and laminated timber — and exports processed EPS under the group's own licence. Australia generates around 45,000 tonnes of polystyrene a year.

Saved from landfill41,580 m³ in 12 months
Per 40 ft container6,930 m³
PlantAustralia's largest
Export licenceWASTE-LIC-2026-V6M6D7 (EPS)
03

Cardboard & paperRecycle North Geelong

Cardboard is separated at the sort line, baled on the group's own balers and conveyors, containerised and exported. Retail, ports and logistics generate it by the tonne; the group turns it into a commodity rather than a cost.

PathwayBale → container → export
EquipmentBalers and conveyors on site
04

Timber & palletsRecycle North Geelong · Timbercycle

Pallets, dunnage, fit-out timber and plain timber are separated on site and recovered for reprocessing.

SourcesPallets, dunnage, fit-outs, construction
PathwayRecovery and reprocessing
05

MetalsRecycle North Geelong · The Mattress Recycling Company

Metals come off the sort line and out of every mattress. Baled spring steel and recovered metals are exported as commodities — the group's $8.5 million investment in mattress processing was made to recover 99% of that steel.

Mattress steel a year3,800–9,600 tonnes
PathwayBaled → containerised → exported
06

Green wasteNunjara

Green waste is accepted at the resource recovery centre and directed to the group's own infrastructure at Nunjara, where every inbound load is recorded by material and volume.

PathwayGroup infrastructure
RecordedCompany, vehicle, origin, material, volume
07

Furniture & household goodsRecycle Warehouse · Love Junk

Anything that still has a life goes to reuse before recycling is considered — through Recycle Warehouse, Love Junk, Vogue Vintage and community material-aid partners.

PathwayReuse first
ChannelsRetail, marketplace, charity partners
Accepted at Recycle North Geelong

More than 90 streams under cover, six days a week — including paint, car, domestic and lithium batteries, NOS canisters, fire extinguishers and tyres, accepted broadly to discourage unlawful dumping.

Not accepted, anywhere in the group
  • Asbestos
  • Prescribed waste
  • Hazardous chemicals
  • Firearms and explosives

These never enter the group's figures. Residual is reported in full rather than netted off.
