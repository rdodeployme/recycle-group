Scale

The last twelve months, at a size you can picture.

The numbers below are the group's own figures. The comparisons are there because a tonne is hard to see.

250k+
Mattresses a year
Laid flat, a year of mattresses would cover the MCG playing surface 23 to 57 times over — a stack six to fourteen metres deep.Range reflects 144,000–360,000 mattresses a year at 12,000–30,000 a month; MCG surface ≈19,600 m².
3,800–9,600t
Mattress steel recovered a year
Between half and one-and-a-third Eiffel Towers of steel, baled and exported as a commodity every year.The Eiffel Tower's iron structure weighs about 7,300 tonnes.
41,580m³
Polystyrene saved from landfill
About 17 Olympic swimming pools of expanded polystyrene, densified to a fraction of the size and reprocessed.An Olympic pool holds about 2,500 m³. Since 1 August; equivalent to six 40 ft containers of processed EPS.
96%
Diverted from landfill

Recovered tonnes over received tonnes, measured at the weighbridge.

11,500–28,800 t
Mattress material diverted a year

Steel, foam, timber and fibre kept out of the ground.

50%+

A day, shredder capacity
