Reporting

Numbers a board can sign off on.

Every load tracked. Every figure reported. Councils and commercial partners receive diversion statements they can put in front of an auditor, a councillor or a shareholder.

Reported through Yarta

Measure. Understand. Report. Improve.

Yarta is Recycle Group's environmental sustainability report and live dashboard for councils and businesses. It evaluates your data against the eco standards and the benchmarks for your industry, compares it with your targets and your last period, then gives you a roadmap to improvement. Where your material passes through Recycle Group, the recycling line is evidenced by the recycler.

Sample screens · Demo Shire Council is fictional · illustrative data

  1. 01Evaluate.

    Send the data you already hold. Yarta enters it, grades the evidence and evaluates it against eco standards with one documented method.

  2. 02Benchmark and compare.

    One score, benchmarked against the eco standards and the published benchmarks for your industry, and compared with your targets and your last period. Your data is never shared with anyone else.

  3. 03Roadmap to improvement.

    Suggestions ranked by what would move the number most, the score you could reach, and the help you can book to get there.

Why believe the number

The recycling line is evidenced by the recycler.

For material that passes through Recycle Group, the waste category on your report rests on the processor's own weighbridge and processing records, not a contractor's summary. Incoming material is separated into more than 90 streams, so each stream can be weighed and traced to a pathway. Material handled by any other contractor is reported too, graded by the evidence behind it and flagged on the report.

The problem

The data is scattered. The score doesn't exist.

Bills, fuel cards, contractor reports, fleet registers, planting records. Every organisation holds the data, and almost none can say where it stands against the eco standards, what would move the number, or what to do first. Recycling has been sold as a collection service and reported as a percentage. It should be a measurable outcome.

Understand

Ten categories. One score.

The Yindyamarra Score is the average of the categories you report. Each one is scored 0 to 100 against the eco standard or your own target, from the figures behind it. A category you don't report is left out, not scored zero.

  1. 01Energy & RenewablesElectricity, gas, solar and batteries. Renewable share and use against your baseline.
  2. 02Fleet & TransportFuel, kilometres, electrification, charging and flights.
  3. 03Carbon & EmissionsScope 1, 2 and reported Scope 3 with NGA factors. Avoided emissions shown separately, never netted.
  4. 04WaterPotable use per bed, student, site or resident, and the share from recycled, rain and stormwater.
  5. 05Waste & Resource RecoveryEvery stream, from kerbside to clinical: recovered, landfilled or treated. Diversion and contamination.
  6. 06Circular EconomyRecycled-content procurement, reuse, packaging and take-back programs.
  7. 07Land & SoilLand rehabilitated, contaminated land remediated, soil reused.
  8. 08Biodiversity & NatureTrees planted and native vegetation, habitat and wetland restored or protected.
  9. 09Community & ParticipationPrograms, events and the people who take part.
  10. 10Governance & ImprovementData completeness, evidence behind each figure, complaints closed, projects delivered.

The same ten categories, shaped by what your sector reports: councils, business and offices, hospitals and aged care, universities and schools, manufacturing, construction and property, retail and hospitality, logistics and transport. What each sector reports on

How it runs

Six steps. One of them is yours.

You send the data. Yarta does the entering, the grading and the calculating. The loop runs every period, and closes when the report issues with its roadmap.

  1. 01Onboard

    Thirty days. Yarta agrees your categories, your sites, your targets and your baseline year with you, and issues the method sheet that states how each one will be counted.

  2. 02Send your data

    Waste and recycling reports, utility bills, fuel and fleet records, invoices, contractor reports. Whatever you already hold for the period.

  3. 03Yarta enters and grades

    Every entry carries an evidence reference and a grade, so any figure on your dashboard can be traced back to the document it came from.

  4. 04Your dashboard moves

    As the data arrives, every tile recalculates: the ten category scores, the score at the eco standards, and your emissions by scope with avoided emissions beside them.

  5. 05Fill in the gaps

    Once a month an email lists what Yarta still needs, by category. A category with no data is marked "not reported" and left out of the score, never guessed.

  6. 06Report issues, with the roadmap

    Monthly, quarterly or annual. Every figure is stated with the method version it was calculated under, and the report closes with the roadmap to improvement.

Method

How the numbers are produced.

Measured, not modelled

Every vehicle is weighed in and out at a Recycle Group transfer station. Loads are split at the sorting line and each stream is weighed again before it leaves. The diversion rate is recovered tonnes divided by received tonnes.

Why we can show it

Recycle Group owns collection, transfer, processing and reuse, so material stays inside the group until it becomes something else. That is what makes the number verifiable.

What we exclude

Asbestos and hazardous waste are not accepted and never enter these figures. Residual is reported in full rather than netted off.

Assurance

Registered, certified, checked every shift.

ISO 9001, 14001 and 45001, EPA Victoria registration and the group's own daily safety system, eCheck.
