Service · Mobile picking stations

Sort it where it lands.

A mobile picking station is a sorting line that comes to you. Mixed loads go over the belt, recyclable streams are picked off, and only what's left goes to landfill. Available to councils and businesses for hire, short term or long term.

How it works

Mixed in. Streams out.

The station is a trailer-mounted sorting deck with a feed hopper, conveyor and chutes to bins below. It needs a flat pad, a loader and people on the belt.

01

Set up on your site

We deliver the station and position it where the loads arrive: a transfer station floor, a hard-waste depot or a works yard.

02

Load goes over the belt

A loader feeds the hopper. Mixed material travels past the pickers at a steady pace.

03

Streams picked off

Cardboard, timber, metals, plastics and other recyclables drop through chutes into their own bins.

04

Residual only to landfill

What's left at the end of the belt is what goes to landfill. The streams go to recycling, through Recycle's own facilities.

Hire terms

Short term or long term.

Hire a station for a campaign, or keep one on site as a standing sorting line.

Short term

A campaign, a clean-up, a backlog

Hard-waste collections, storm and dumping clean-ups, event waste, or a stockpile that has built up. The station arrives, the backlog is sorted, the station leaves.

Long term

A standing line at your site

For a transfer station or a business with a steady mixed stream. The station stays, and your diversion rate changes for good.

Why hire one

Landfill diversion without the capital outlay.

A sorting line is the single biggest lever a transfer station or a business has on its diversion rate. Hiring one means you don't have to build it.

Councils

Transfer stations, hard-waste programs, illegal dumping clean-ups and storm recovery. Sort on site, send less to landfill, and report the difference.

ResultMeasured diversion

Businesses

Warehouses, manufacturers, builders and demolition contractors with a mixed stream that currently goes in one bin.

ResultLower landfill cost

No build, no lock-in

The station arrives on a trailer and leaves the same way. Scale up for a campaign, scale down after it.

ResultFlexible capacity

Streams go into the group

What's picked off goes to Recycle's own processing: cardboard baled, timber recovered, metals and polystyrene processed.

ResultOne chain of custody
