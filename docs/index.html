Why we innovate

Australia can't burn its way out of waste.

China and much of Europe send huge volumes of waste to incinerators for energy. In Australia, waste-to-energy is tightly restricted — so the answer has to be recycling, and more than one way of doing it. That is what Recycle Group builds.

How it works

Collected, processed, back on the market.

Every load follows the same path through Recycle Group's own sites — and every step is weighed and recorded.

  1. 01CollectOur crews and fleet, on your schedule.
  2. 02Weigh inEvery load weighed on arrival.
  3. 03SeparateSorted into 90+ streams under cover.
  4. 04ProcessBaled, densified, shredded or stripped down.
  5. 05Back to marketCommodities sold and exported; goods reused.
  6. 06ReportWeighed out and reported by site, stream and period.
