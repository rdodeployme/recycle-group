What Recycle Group does

Collection is the easy part.

Knowing what happened to everything afterwards is where Recycle Group begins. Material is collected, weighed, separated, processed into commodities or returned to use, and every stream is reported — because collection, transfer, processing and reuse all sit inside the group.

Landfill last

Four questions, always in this order.

Reuse first. Then recycle. Then recover what can be separated. Only what fails all three goes to landfill — and even that is weighed and recorded.
