Why we innovate

Australia can't burn its way out of waste.

China and much of Europe send huge volumes of waste to incinerators for energy. In Australia, waste-to-energy is tightly restricted — so the answer has to be recycling, from the mainstream to the complex. That is what Recycle Group builds.

China, 2024
84.6%of municipal waste incinerated, across 1,129 waste-to-energy plants.

Our operations · Australia-wide

Eight recycling operations, built to run anywhere in Australia.

01Mattress recycling facility
  • About 38 seconds per mattress
  • Twin-shaft shredders — up to 1,400 m³ of product a day
  • Spring steel separated by magnets, vibration, suction and an electrostatic fibre separator — 99% free of fabric
02Australia's fastest-growing polystyrene plant
  • 300+ m³ a day through five hot-melt densifiers and a cold press
  • Three shipping containers of foam into one cube the size of a washing machine
  • Exported under the group's own licence
03Resource recovery centres
  • 90+ streams separated under cover, six days a week
  • Public and trade drop-off
  • Batteries, paint, tyres and fire extinguishers accepted — so they aren't dumped
04Cardboard recovery
  • Conveyors and balers on site
  • Baled and containerised
  • Exported as a commodity
05Metal recycling
  • Steel and coloured metals sorted and baled
  • Mattress spring steel supplied to international buyers
  • Exported as commodities
06Timber recovery
  • Pallets, dunnage and plain timber
  • Separated from mixed loads
  • Recovered for reprocessing
07Shredding
  • Shredding and size reduction of recovered material
  • Residual cut to a quarter of its original volume
  • Less landfill space, better compaction
08Organics
  • Food, green and organic waste
  • Commingled and organic services for port precincts
  • Green waste to the group's own infrastructure
