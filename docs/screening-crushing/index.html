Service · Contract screening and crushing

Screen it. Crush it. Use it again.

Recycle screens and crushes concrete, brick, rock, soil and demolition material at our own facilities or on your site. We bring the plant and the people: loaders, dozers, excavators, screens, crushers and stockpilers. You get graded product back, and a measured result.

Two ways to run it

At our facilities, or at yours.

Some material is worth bringing to us. Some is better crushed where it sits, so it never leaves the site and comes straight back into the job.

01

At a Recycle facility

Bring concrete, rock, brick and clean fill to our sites. It is weighed in, screened and crushed to spec, and sold or returned as recycled product. Every load is recorded.

Our sites
02

On your site

We mobilise a full crushing and screening spread to your project, with the operators to run it. Demolition rubble, excavated rock and stockpiled fill become road base, aggregate and engineered fill without a truck movement off site.

Contract crushing

We provide everything. You provide the material.

A contract job means Recycle supplies the whole spread and the labour to run it. Nothing to hire separately, nothing to coordinate between suppliers.

01

Assess

Material, volume, site access and the product you need at the end: road base, aggregate, screened fill or a mix.

02

Mobilise

Crushers, screens, stockpilers, loaders, dozers and excavators arrive with the crew that runs them.

03

Crush and screen

Material is processed to specification and stockpiled by grade, ready to use or to sell.

04

Report

Volumes processed and product produced are recorded, so the job closes with numbers, not estimates.
