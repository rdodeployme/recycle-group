The requirement

Government sites, fleets and facilities generate the same streams as any large organisation, with a higher bar for evidence.

Recycle Group runs the program under its own ISO 9001, 14001 and 45001 systems and EPA Victoria registration, and reports it the way an auditor expects to read it.

At the facilityEvery load is weighed in and out at a Recycle Group facility; the diversion rate is recovered tonnes over received tonnes.
