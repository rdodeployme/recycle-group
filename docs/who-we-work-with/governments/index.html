The requirement

Government sites, fleets and facilities generate the same streams as any large organisation, with a higher bar for evidence. Recycle Group runs the program under its own ISO 9001, 14001 and 45001 systems and EPA Victoria registration, and reports it the way an auditor expects to read it.

Every load is weighed in and out at a Recycle Group facility; the diversion rate is recovered tonnes over received tonnes.

What the program includes
  • Facility and depot clearances
  • Scheduled collections across multiple sites
  • Data-bearing asset handling on request
  • Program design against a specification
  • Reporting to the agency's cadence
What you receive
  • Collection statements per site
  • Program diversion reports
  • Certificates and registrations with the proposal
  • A single point of contact
