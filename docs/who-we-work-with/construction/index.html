Recycle Group / Sectors / Tunnel & infrastructure construction
The requirement

Large infrastructure projects bring in heavy plant and components from around the world, protected in polystyrene, timber crating and steel.

Recycle Group works with the project to collect that packaging, densify the polystyrene at its own plant and recover the timber and steel, so a project's packaging doesn't become its landfill problem.

At the facilityPolystyrene is densified at the group's own plant and exported under its licence.

A major tunnel construction project

The brief

The project's equipment arrived packed in expanded polystyrene. Loose EPS is almost all air: it fills bins in an hour, blows around a site and takes up landfill out of all proportion to its weight.

What we did

  • Built a way to recover all of the project's polystyrene packaging rather than skip it
  • Densified it at Australia's fastest-growing polystyrene plant
  • Turned it into dense polymer that goes back into manufacturing
100%of the project's polystyrene recycledNone of it sent to landfill
