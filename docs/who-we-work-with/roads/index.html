Recycle Group / Sectors / State road operators
The requirement

Road corridors collect what people leave behind and what works generate.

Recycle Group clears rest areas, verges and dumping sites, recovers timber, metal and rubble, and reports the program by corridor or region so the operator can show what was recovered rather than what was tipped.

At the facilityEvery load is weighed in and out; recovered timber and metal leave the group as commodities.

Designed around the requirement.
