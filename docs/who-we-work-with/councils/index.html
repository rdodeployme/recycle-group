The requirement

Councils come to Recycle Group for hard-waste collections, bulk clean-ups after events or illegal dumping, transfer station operation and material processing.

Because collection, transfer and processing all sit inside the group, the diversion figure a council publishes is measured at the weighbridge rather than estimated from a conversion table, and residual is reported in full.

At the facilityThe resource recovery centres accept 90+ streams under cover, six days a week, with up to ten free recyclable categories and a resident discount across Geelong, Queenscliff, Surf Coast, Golden Plains, Wyndham and Colac Otway.

Councils · Victoria · FY2025–26

A council's mattresses and couches, recovered across two transfer stations.

A Victorian coastal shire council

The brief

The council needed mattresses and couches dropped at two of its transfer stations taken out of the landfill stream, with the outcome reported back for the year.

What we did

  • Took mattresses and couches from both transfer stations through the year
  • Stripped every item at The Mattress Recycling Company: spring steel and timber recovered, the residual shredded
  • Issued a Recycling Outcomes Report for the financial year, broken out by site and by item size
4,394mattresses and couches in one year2,744 mattresses and 1,650 couches
