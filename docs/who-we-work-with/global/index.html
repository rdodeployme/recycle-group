The requirement

Recycle Group already exports what it recovers: baled mattress steel and metals as commodities, and processed polystyrene under the group's own export licence.

For international partners the offer is the same chain of custody, from a site in Australia to a commodity landed overseas, with the outcome reported at each step.

Spring steel to export
Export · trans-global

Mattress spring steel, baled and exported as a commodity.

International steel buyers

The brief

Around a third of a mattress by weight is steel. Victoria was landfilling 160,000 mattresses a year, and the steel went with them.

What we did

  • Process 12,000 to 30,000 mattresses a month at The Mattress Recycling Company
  • Shred and magnetically separate the spring steel to about 99% ferrous
  • Bale it on our own baler and load containers on site for direct export
