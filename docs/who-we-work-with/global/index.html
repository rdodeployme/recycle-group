The requirement

Recycle Group already exports what it recovers — baled mattress steel and metals as commodities, and processed polystyrene under the group's own export licence. For international partners the offer is the same chain of custody, from a site in Australia to a commodity landed overseas, with the outcome reported at each step.

Waste export licence WASTE-LIC-2026-V6M6D7 covers expanded polystyrene, including densified and reprocessed material.

What the program includes
  • Processed polystyrene export (licensed)
  • Baled steel and metal export
  • Containerised cardboard
  • Commodity supply to international manufacturers
  • Recovery programs for international organisations operating in Australia
What you receive
  • Chain-of-custody documentation
  • Outcome by stream and shipment
  • Diversion reporting for the partner's own disclosures
