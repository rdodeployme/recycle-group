Recycle Group / Sectors / Property developers
The requirement

Development sites generate clean fill and topsoil in volume — material that shouldn't be dumped.

When a Victorian property developer had 60,000 m³ of topsoil to discard, Recycle Group took it on, and 20,000 m³ of it went back into use as beautification. Every load is weighed and recorded, and the outcome is reported for the project.

At the facilityClean fill only — contaminated soil, asbestos and prescribed waste are not accepted at any Recycle Group facility.
