Recycle Group / Sectors / Property developers
The requirement

Development sites generate clean fill and topsoil in volume, material that shouldn't be dumped.

When a Victorian property developer had 135,000 m³ of topsoil to discard, Recycle Group took it on, and 20,000 m³ of it went back into use as beautification. Every load is weighed and recorded, and the outcome is reported for the project.

At the facilityClean fill only. Contaminated soil, asbestos and prescribed waste are not accepted at any Recycle Group facility.

Property developers · Victoria

135,000 m³ of topsoil a developer was going to discard.

A Victorian property developer

The brief

A development site had 135,000 cubic metres of topsoil to get rid of. Sent to landfill, a usable material would have been lost.

What we did

  • Took on the full 135,000 m³ instead of it being dumped or landfilled
  • Recycled the material as clean fill and topsoil
  • Returned 20,000 m³ to the project for beautification and landscaping
