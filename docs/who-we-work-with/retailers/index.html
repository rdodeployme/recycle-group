Recycle Group / Sectors / National solutions for retailers
The requirement

Retail generates cardboard by the tonne, polystyrene by the truckload, and fit-out waste every time a store refreshes. Recycle Group runs the program across the network, recovers packaging into commodities through its own baling and polystyrene plant, and reports each store's outcome so the network figure is a sum, not an estimate.

The group runs Australia's largest polystyrene plant and its own cardboard baling and containerisation.

What the program includes
  • Cardboard and packaging recovery programs
  • Polystyrene collection and densification
  • Store fit-out, refit and closure clearances
  • Fixtures and fittings reuse
  • Scheduled runs across a store network
What you receive
  • Per-store collection statements
  • Network diversion reports
  • Commodity outcome by stream
  • Inputs for retailer sustainability reporting
