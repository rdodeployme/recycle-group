Airports

Airport precincts run around the clock, and so does their waste. Commingled, food and organic waste, freight packaging and polystyrene, recovered and reported.

Recycle Group / Sectors / Airports
The requirement

Terminals, freight sheds and airside operations generate commingled recycling, food and organic waste, cardboard, pallets and polystyrene from air freight.

Recycle Group separates it at its resource recovery centres, densifies the polystyrene at its own plant, bales the cardboard and reports the outcome for the airport's sustainability targets.

At the facilityAsbestos, prescribed waste and hazardous chemicals are not accepted at any Recycle Group facility.

Your streams, separated

One mixed load. Every stream recovered.

The three streams we see most from airports, sorted out of a mixed load. Whatever cannot be recovered is reported, not hidden.

What the program includes

Designed around the requirement.

  1. 01Commingled recycling across terminals
  2. 02Food and organic waste
  3. 03Cardboard and freight packaging
  4. 04Pallets and polystyrene from air freight
  5. 05Furniture, metals, e-waste and bulky goods from terminal refurbishments
  6. 06Collections scheduled to airside access windows
