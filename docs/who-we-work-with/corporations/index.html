Recycle Group / Sectors / Corporations
The requirement

Multi-site businesses lose the thread when every site has its own contractor.

Recycle Group designs the program once, runs the collections with its own crews and fleet, processes the material in its own facilities and reports by site, region and period, so head office sees one number and the story behind it.

At the facilityCardboard is baled and containerised for export; metals are recovered and exported as commodities; polystyrene is densified at the group's own plant.

Your streams, separated

One mixed load. Every stream recovered.

The three streams we see most from corporations, sorted out of a mixed load. Whatever cannot be recovered is reported, not hidden.

What the program includes

Designed around the requirement.

  1. 01Office, warehouse and back-of-house clearances
  2. 02Scheduled collections across a network
  3. 03Fit-out and end-of-lease programs
  4. 04Cardboard, timber, metal and polystyrene recovery
  5. 05Furniture and fittings reused where possible
