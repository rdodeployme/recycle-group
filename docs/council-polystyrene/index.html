Service · On-site polystyrene densifying for councils

90 cubic metres in. One cubic metre out.

We put a hot-melt polystyrene densifier on your transfer station. Your staff feed it loose polystyrene as it comes through the gate. We collect the dense ingots and recycle them. Nothing goes to landfill.

How it works

Your site does the shrinking.

Polystyrene is up to 98% air. Moving it loose is moving air. Densifying it where it arrives takes the volume out before a truck is ever called.

01

We install the densifier

A hot-melt unit delivered to your transfer station, commissioned, with your staff trained to run it.

02

Council processes on site

Staff feed clean, loose polystyrene into the hopper. The machine shreds it, melts it at about 220°C and presses it into solid ingots.

03

We collect the ingots

Ingots stack in IBCs we supply. One IBC holds what was about 90 m³ of loose foam. When it's full, we collect it.

04

Recycled into new product

The ingots go through Recycle's polystyrene plant and come back as picture frames, skirting boards, insulation and packaging.

The numbers

Why it changes the economics.

The volume reduction happens before transport, so the cost of moving polystyrene drops with it.

90:1
Volume reduction, on site

Ninety cubic metres of loose foam becomes about one cubic metre of ingots. Thirty 3 m³ bags, or a semi-trailer load, into one IBC.

Machine

Output

What goes in
  • Clean expanded polystyrene (EPS)
  • Packaging foam

  • Commercial polystyrene
Keep out
  • Contaminated or wet foam
  • Food-soiled packaging
  • Other plastics

Why councils do it

The foam problem, solved at the gate.

Polystyrene is the stream that fills a bin in hours, blows around a site and costs a fortune to cart. Densifying it on site ends all three.

No more carting air

One collection replaces dozens. The truck comes for a full IBC of ingots, not a trailer of loose foam.

ResultTransport cost down

Your staff, your pace

The machine runs when polystyrene comes in. No scheduling around a collection window.

ResultFits the site

A tidier site

Loose foam doesn't pile up or blow across the yard. It goes into the hopper as it arrives.

ResultCleaner transfer station

Counted and reported

Every IBC collected is weighed and reported back, so the council can publish the tonnes it kept out of landfill.
