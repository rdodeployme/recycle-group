How it works

Four steps. One line.

Every rigid product takes the same path on the group's own site: from whole item, to shred, to small even regrind, to bulk bags in a shipping container.

01

Shred

Single-shaft shredder

A hydraulic ram pushes whole items against one slow-turning rotor fitted with rows of cutting teeth. A screen keeps the plastic in the cutting chamber until it's small enough to pass. Bulky, thick-walled plastic is what it's built for.

In
Whole rigid items, metal off
Out
Coarse shred
02

Granulate

High-speed granulator

Knives on a fast-spinning rotor cut the shred against fixed knives in the housing until it passes through the screen. The screen sets the size, and even-sized regrind flows, packs and handles far better than shred.

In
Coarse shred
Out
Regrind
03

Bag

Bulk bags

Regrind is packed into bulk bags: big woven bags with lifting loops. A forklift lifts each full bag by its loops, so the regrind is stacked, stored and moved without any loose material.

In
Regrind
Out
Full bulk bags
04

Load

Shipping containers

Full bulk bags are loaded into shipping containers. A container keeps the bags together, covered and secure, and ready to move as one load.

In
Full bulk bags
Out
Loaded containers

What comes out
HDPEHDPE regrindResin code 2

From bins, pallets, IBCs, crates, milk crates and pipe. Can go into new bins, pipe, pallets and crates.

PPPP regrindResin code 5

From bumpers, car plastics, pallets, crates, baskets and chairs. Can go into automotive and industrial mouldings, crates and pallets.

PERotomoulded PE regrindResin code 2 or 4

From tanks and barriers. Can go into pipe, pallets, and garden and building products.

LLDPEBaled filmResin code 4 · baled, not shredded

Film wraps around shredder rotors, so pallet wrap is baled and sent to a film recycler for new film, bin liners and bags.

Why it matters

Most plastic still isn't recovered.
