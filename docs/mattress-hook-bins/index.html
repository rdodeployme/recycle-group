Service · Mobile mattress recycling points

Drop off. Council fills. We collect.

A 40 m³ caged hook bin built for mattresses. We set it down on a council site, staff fill it at their own pace, and a hook truck lifts it away. No loading crew on site, no double handling.

How it works

Three steps. Nobody loads a truck by hand.

The bin stays on site while it fills. Collection is a hook truck and a lift, so the heavy work happens once, at our end.

01

We drop it off

Our hook truck sets the bin down on the council site, at a spot with room for the truck to return and lift it.

02

Council fills it

Staff put mattresses in as they come in, when it suits them. The caged sides show how full it is at a glance.

03

We hook it and go

When it's full, we hook it, lift it and swap it out. The load goes to The Mattress Recycling Company, which recovers 99% of each mattress.

Why councils use it

Less lifting. Cleaner loads. One truck to collect.

Mattresses are bulky, awkward and slow to load by hand. The hook bin moves that work off the council site.

Less manual handling

Staff load mattresses one at a time as they arrive. There's no two-person loading job when the bin is collected.

ResultSafer for staff

Filled on your terms

The bin sits on site until it's full. Council decides when mattresses go in, not a collection schedule.

ResultYour own pace

Visible and ventilated

Caged sides let air through and let anyone check the fill level or the contents from the ground.

ResultEasy visual checks

Secure on the road

Solid roof, solid back and locking doors keep the load contained while it travels.

ResultContained in transit

One stream, kept clean

Signed for mattresses only, so general waste stays out and the load arrives ready for processing.

ResultStraight to recovery
