Who we collect from

Wherever the old equipment is.

Councils clear depots and parks. Road crews replace guardrail and signs. Households clear garages. Offices strip out floors. Warehouses change their racking. Hospitals, villages and campuses replace equipment. Factories, farms, kitchens, solar sites, airports and ports upgrade. Most of what comes out is metal, and none of it should go to landfill.

How Recycle Group reports
Done properly

Data, cleared equipment, clear limits.

A few things are settled before the truck arrives, so the collection is safe and the record is clean.

Data

Drives out before we come

Removed or wiped by your team and confirmed in a signed declaration. We do not take loose drives.

Data-bearing equipment → Medical

Cleared equipment only
