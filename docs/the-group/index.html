Programs & initiatives

Programs that keep material out of landfill and off the roadside.

Illegal roadside dumping

In development

Residents report dumped waste through no.com.au. It is collected next day and recycled rather than landfilled, and public reporting puts dumpers under scrutiny.

DeliversDumping response & awarenessno.com.au

Free recycling streams

Making common recyclables free to drop off has significantly reduced illegal roadside dumping. Free at the transfer station: whitegoods, clean scrap metal, cardboard, polystyrene, printer cartridges, selected e-waste, paint (with Paintback) and car batteries.
