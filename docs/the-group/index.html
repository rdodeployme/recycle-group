One group. Six roles.

Every business in the group has a defined role in the chain of custody — collect, recover, reuse and community, report, landfill, or systems. This is the one page where they are all laid out.

The structure

Six roles. One chain of custody.

Material enters through the collection brands and never leaves the group until it is something else — reused, recycled, recovered or, last, landfilled and recorded.
