The role

This is the physical core of the group. Recycle North Geelong is a purpose-built resource recovery centre and public transfer station where material is weighed in, separated into more than 90 streams under cover and weighed out. The Mattress Recycling Company strips a mattress in about 38 seconds — steel, foam, timber and fibre — at a rate of 12,000 to 30,000 a month. Styro runs Australia's largest polystyrene plant, densifying expanded polystyrene for reprocessing and licensed export. Timbercycle recovers timber.
