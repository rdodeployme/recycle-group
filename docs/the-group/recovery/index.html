The role

This is the physical core of the group.

The Recycle Resource Recovery Centres are purpose-built resource recovery centres and public transfer stations, run to one standard, and designed so the group can run other operators' transfer stations the same way. At each one material is weighed in, separated into more than 90 streams under cover and weighed out. The Mattress Recycling Company strips a mattress into steel, foam, timber and fibre in about 38 seconds, at a rate of 12,000 to 30,000 a month. Styro runs Australia's fastest-growing polystyrene plant, densifying expanded polystyrene for reprocessing and licensed export. Timbercycle recovers timber, Shred reduces recovered material for processing, and Global Steel supplies recovered steel to market.
