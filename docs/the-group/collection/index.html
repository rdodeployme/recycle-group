The role

Collection is where the group meets the customer.

JUNK clears houses, offices and sites in Melbourne and Brisbane; Trash. books the items that are too big, too heavy or too awkward online at a fixed price; Rubbish gives customers multiple fixed-price quotes from local registered collectors; Declutter re-organises homes, run by women, for women. Every one of them brings material into the group's own chain of custody instead of a tip, which is what makes the group's diversion figure a measured number rather than an estimate.
