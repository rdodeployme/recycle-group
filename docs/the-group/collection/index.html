The role

Collection is where the group meets the customer. JUNK clears houses, offices and sites in Melbourne and Brisbane; Trash. books bulky household items online with a price up front; Rubbish runs same-day residential collections; Declutter re-organises homes, run by women, for women. Every one of them brings material into the group's own chain of custody instead of a tip — which is what makes the group's diversion figure a measured number rather than an estimate.
