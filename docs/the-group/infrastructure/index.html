The role

Nunjara is the group's own landfill site, the end of the chain, used last.

Its material load register records every inbound load by company, vehicle, origin, material and volume, so even what reaches landfill is counted rather than assumed. Langi Ghiran Recycling is also part of the group's landfill-last capacity. EPA Victoria registrations R000314340 and R000314341.
