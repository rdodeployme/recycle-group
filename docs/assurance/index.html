Assurance

Safe, registered, checked every shift.

What sits behind a Recycle Group program: certified management systems, EPA registration, and the group's own daily safety system used on every site.

Systems & registrations
  • ISO 9001:2015: quality management
  • ISO 14001:2015: environmental management
  • ISO 45001:2018: occupational health and safety
  • AS/NZS 5377: e-waste collection, storage, transport and treatment
  • NIST 800-88: media sanitisation for data-bearing assets
  • EPA Victoria: registration R000312600 (North Geelong site); R000314340 and R000314341 (Nunjara)
  • Waste export licence WASTE-LIC-2026-V6M6D7 for expanded polystyrene
eCheck: every shift, every site

eCheck is the group's own safety, training, policy and compliance system, built in-house and used daily at North Geelong.

  • Daily staff check: fit for work, drug and alcohol free, not impaired by medication, emergency contact, licence or permit
  • Plant and vehicle pre-starts tied to a fleet ID, with an hour-meter reading and photo
  • Fault and incident loop: report → manager follow-up → action → signature
  • About 58 check types across eight groups, including high-risk and urgent checks
  • 62 policies across retail, transport, transfer station, recycling and office
  • Training modules for mattress processing, polystyrene processing, lifting and tailgate lifters

Asset compliance

Every machine and vehicle is checked before it runs, against its own ID, with the meter reading captured.

Staff compliance

Every operator declares fitness for work each shift; training records sit against the person.

Work compliance

Site checks, high-risk checks and end-of-day forms close the loop, with a manager sign-off on every fault.
