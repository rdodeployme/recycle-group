What we take on

One operator for the whole site.

A council or operator hands over the site. Recycle Group runs it, recovers the material through its own facilities and reports the result.

Site operation

Staffing, the gate, opening hours and the public and trade drop-off, run day to day by Recycle Group.

ResultThe whole site

Weighbridge records

Every load weighed in and weighed out. Every figure we report starts at the weighbridge ticket.

ResultMeasured, not estimated

Separation

Material separated into more than 90 streams on site, under cover, so more of it can be recovered.

Result90+ streams

Processing in the group

Mattresses, polystyrene, timber, metals and cardboard go straight into Recycle Group's own processing, not a broker.

ResultOur own facilities

Safety & compliance

Daily pre-start checks, training records and incident reporting through eCheck, on EPA-registered sites.

ResulteCheck · EPA registered

Reporting

Diversion statements by site and by period that a council can put in front of an auditor or a councillor.

ResultThrough Yarta
How a load moves

Weighed in. Separated. Weighed out.

Every truck crosses the weighbridge on the way in and on the way out. What it leaves behind is tipped into its own bay, one of more than 90 streams.

Live modelA truck crosses the weighbridge, tips into a bay and is weighed again on the way out.
What residents and trade can bring

Take more in. Keep more out of landfill.

A broad acceptance list gives people somewhere to take things, which is how a transfer station cuts dumping and lifts recovery.

Accepted · 90+ streams, including
  • Metal
  • Coloured metals
  • Cardboard & paper
  • Plastics
  • Polystyrene
  • Timber & pallets
  • Organics
  • Commingled
  • Mattresses
  • Clean fill & soil
  • E-waste & batteries
  • Tyres, paint & more
Not accepted at any Recycle Group facility
  • Asbestos
  • Prescribed waste
  • Hazardous chemicals
