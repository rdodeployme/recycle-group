Solutions

A recycling program, end to end.

Most operators collect and hand off, so the chain of custody ends at the gate. Recycle Group owns each step — collection, transfer, processing, reuse and reporting — so the program is designed once and run as one system.

01

Program design & contracts

We map your material streams, sites and volumes, then design a program with the recovery pathway for each stream agreed before it starts.

  • Stream audit
  • Multi-site
  • Recurring or one-off
02

Collection & clearance

Scheduled collections, bulk clearances and hard-waste programs run by the group's own crews and fleet, with the lifting and loading done by us.

  • Hard waste
  • Clearances
  • Scheduled runs
03

Transfer & sorting

Every load is weighed in, separated into more than 90 material streams on site, and weighed again before it leaves — the basis of every figure we report.

  • Weighbridge
  • 90+ streams
  • Undercover
04

Material processing

Mattresses, polystyrene, timber, cardboard, metals and green waste processed in the group's own facilities and recycled back into manufacturing as commodities.

  • Mattresses
  • EPS
  • Timber
  • Cardboard
  • Metal
05

Reuse & community redistribution

Furniture and goods that still have a life are rehomed through the group's reuse operations and community partners before recycling is considered.

  • Reuse first
  • Material aid
  • Retail
06

Reporting & evidence

Diversion statements per collection, site and program — recovered tonnes over received tonnes, with residual published rather than netted off.

  • Diversion rate
  • By stream
  • By site
How it works

Six steps. One chain of custody.

Nothing here is “away”. Material moves from your site through the group's own infrastructure and out the other side as a commodity, a reused item or a published residual figure.

1

Site

Streams, volumes and sites mapped. Recovery pathway agreed per stream.

2

Collection

Group crews and fleet collect, lift and load. Scheduled or on call.

3

Transfer & sort

Weighed in. Separated into 90+ streams under cover. Weighed out.

4

Processing

Mattresses, EPS, timber, cardboard and metal processed in group facilities.

5

Reuse & recycling

Goods rehomed; materials recycled back into manufacturing as commodities.

6

Reporting

Diversion statement issued. Residual reported in full. Landfill last.

Measured, not modelled

The diversion rate is recovered tonnes divided by received tonnes. No estimates, no conversion factors.

Why we can show it

Collection, transfer, processing and reuse are all in the group, so material stays inside the chain of custody until it becomes something else.

What we exclude

Asbestos and hazardous waste are not accepted and never enter these figures. Residual is reported in full rather than netted off.
